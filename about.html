About Vandana Travel Experts

Vandana Travel Experts was established in 2017 by Mr. Manish Mishra, a seasoned travel professional with over 25 years of experience in the aviation and travel industry. Before founding the company, he worked with leading organizations including Sahara Airlines, Jetlite, Amadeus, Kingfisher Airlines and HDFC Bank — gaining extensive expertise in airline operations, travel management and customer service.

With deep industry knowledge and strong global partnerships, Vandana Travel Experts has grown into a trusted one-stop travel solution provider catering to both individual travellers and corporate clients — across domestic and international, inbound and outbound travel.

Our story

A One-Stop Travel Solution

Having worked with airlines, GDS and more, Vandana Travel Experts is a one-stop solution for all travel-related services in domestic and international, inbound and outbound travel — from social events to corporate MICE events, corporate ticketing, groups, corporate day trips and weddings, right from décor to planning every section in every department.

Our offices are located in Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra as our head office and Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh as a branch office — the city of Mahakumbh & Sangam — with associate offices in all major tourist destinations across the world. Our specialization is perfectly crafted, personalized itineraries for individuals, groups, incentives and conferences for corporates.

We have an energetic team of thorough professionals who, with their ever-enthusiastic attitude, assist our guests in making the best possible arrangements. We hold an affiliation with IATA TIDS and are also members of several distinguished associations such as TAAI, TAFI, ADTOI & IATO. We are also recognized by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India.

When it comes to planning successful corporate events — Meetings, Incentives, Conferences and Exhibitions (MICE) — it requires a perfect blend of creativity, logistics and execution. With our philosophy of keeping it simple, we don't just organize events; we create unforgettable corporate journeys. To date we have organized events in Goa, Mumbai, Kerala, Alibag, Lonavala, Chennai, Jaipur, Udaipur, Bali, Thailand, Dubai and Sri Lanka.
