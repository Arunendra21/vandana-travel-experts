About Vandana Travel Experts

Vandana Travel Experts, established in 2017 by ex-Airline employee Mr. Manish Mishra — who has worked with Sahara Airlines, Jetlite, Amadeus, Kingfisher Airlines and HDFC Bank — brings 25 years of experience in the travel industry. A rapidly growing travel company, it is redefining the way people travel through innovative solutions, personalized service and seamless experiences across every segment: Corporate, Group, Families, Individuals and MICE travellers.

Having worked with airlines, DMCs, GDS and banks, Mr. Mishra pours all of his experience into Vandana Travel Experts with one simple vision — keeping it simple.

Our story

A One-Stop Travel Solution

Vandana Travel Experts is a one-stop solution for all travel-related services in domestic and international, inbound and outbound travel — from social events to corporate MICE events, corporate ticketing, VISA, apostille and attestation of documents, passports, groups, corporate day trips, cruises and weddings, right from décor to planning every section in every department.

Our offices are located in Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra as our head office and Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh as a branch office — the city of Mahakumbh & Sangam — with associate offices in all major tourist destinations across the world. Our specialization is perfectly crafted, personalized itineraries for individuals, groups, incentives and conferences for corporates. We have an energetic team of thorough professionals who, with their ever-enthusiastic attitude, assist our guests in making the best possible arrangements.

We share sound relations with airlines, tourism boards, hotels and other travel partners. We hold an affiliation with IATA TIDS and are members of several distinguished associations such as TAFI, ADTOI, NIDHI & IATO. We are also recognized by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India.

When it comes to planning successful corporate events — Meetings, Incentives, Conferences and Exhibitions (MICE) — it requires a perfect blend of creativity, logistics and execution. As one of the Top 75 MICE Companies in Mumbai, we don't just organize events; with our philosophy of keeping it simple, we create unforgettable corporate journeys. To date we have organized events in Goa, Kashmir, Mumbai, Kerala, Alibag, Lonavala, Chennai, Varanasi, Jaipur, Udaipur, Bali, Thailand, Dubai and Sri Lanka.
