What is corporate travel management?

One desk for every trip your business takes

Corporate travel management is the coordination of a company's business trips — booking flights and hotels, arranging airport transfers and ground transport, handling visas and travel documents, and supporting travellers before, during and after the journey.

Instead of every employee arranging their own trip, a single travel partner brings it together: consistent bookings, clear quotes, quick changes when plans shift, and one number to call if something goes wrong on the road. That is exactly the service Vandana Travel Experts provides for businesses.
