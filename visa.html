Visa basics

Know Your Visa

A quick guide to the most common visa categories. Our experts help you choose and apply for the right one.

Tourist Visa

For leisure travel, sightseeing and visiting family or friends. A short-stay visa that does not permit work or study in the destination country.

Business Visa

For meetings, conferences, negotiations and short business trips. It allows business activity but not local employment or salaried work.

Employment Visa

For taking up a job or long-term work abroad. Usually requires employer sponsorship, a work contract and supporting documentation.

Student Visa

For enrolling in a recognised college, university or programme abroad. Typically needs an admission letter and proof of funds.

Transit Visa

For passing through a country on the way to your final destination. Rules depend on your layover length, airport and nationality.
