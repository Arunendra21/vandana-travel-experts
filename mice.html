In detail

What each MICE programme includes

Meetings

Corporate Meetings & Leadership Offsites

From quarterly board meetings to multi-day leadership offsites and dealer/franchisee conventions, we manage every aspect so your team can focus on the agenda, not the logistics.

  • Venue sourcing and contracting — hotels, resorts and conference centres
  • Group air and rail travel coordination for all attendees
  • Meeting room setup, seating plans and AV equipment coordination
  • Group accommodation blocks with preferred corporate rates
  • Local transport, airport transfers and welcome desks
  • Catering coordination and dietary requirement management
Incentive travel

Reward Trips That Motivate Your Best People

Incentive travel is one of the most powerful tools for retaining top talent and channel partners. We design memorable domestic and international itineraries that feel premium, run smoothly and reflect your brand.

  • Curated destination options — beach, hill, heritage and international hubs
  • Group flight bookings and charter coordination for large cohorts
  • Themed itineraries with sightseeing, gala dinners and team activities
  • Tier-based packages for different employee or partner categories
  • On-ground tour managers and 24/7 support during the trip
  • Travel insurance and visa processing bundled for all travellers
Conferences

Large-Scale Conferences & Sales Conventions

Annual conferences and sales conventions bring together hundreds of delegates from across the country — and sometimes the world. We manage the full delegate lifecycle so the event runs without a hitch.

  • Delegate registration, travel booking and itinerary management
  • Hotel room blocks across multiple properties with single billing
  • Stage, AV, branding and production vendor coordination
  • Group ground transport — coaches, cabs and airport transfers
  • Dedicated on-site help desks for delegate queries
  • Post-event reports, cost reconciliation and feedback summaries
Exhibitions & trade fairs

Hassle-Free Participation in Exhibitions

Participating in a major trade fair involves coordinating staff travel, stay and logistics around tight exhibition schedules. We take care of the travel and hospitality so your team can focus on the stall and the leads.

  • Group flight and hotel bookings near exhibition venues
  • Coordination with stall and booth logistics partners
  • Staff visa processing for international exhibitions
  • Local transport between hotel, venue and airport
  • Travel insurance for all participating staff
  • Flexible booking changes for last-minute schedule shifts
