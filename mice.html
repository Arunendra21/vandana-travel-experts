What is MICE?

Meetings, Incentives, Conferences & Events

MICE is a specialised part of travel focused on bringing groups together for a purpose — a team meeting, a reward trip for high performers, an industry conference or a company event. It combines event planning with group travel: the destination and venue, the logistics of moving and hosting a group, and the coordination that makes the day run smoothly.

Vandana Travel Experts plans and delivers MICE programmes end to end, so a single team looks after your event, your group's travel and everything in between.
